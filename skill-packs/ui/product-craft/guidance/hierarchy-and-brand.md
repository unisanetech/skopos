# Hierarchy And Brand

1. Identify the user's primary job and the minimum information needed to complete it.
2. Load the project-bound brand doctrine, semantic tokens, and component catalog before
   choosing local visual values.
3. Establish hierarchy through order, type, spacing, and emphasis before adding
   containers, borders, shadows, gradients, or illustrations.
4. Reuse an existing component only when its behavior and information role match.
5. Treat existing screens as precedents only when the project binding identifies them
   as approved and explains why.

Reject an interface that puts every region in a card, uses decoration to compensate for
weak hierarchy, invents local tokens, or copies a visually attractive screen whose
architecture or product purpose differs from the task.
